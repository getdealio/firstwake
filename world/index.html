The Crownless Reach

A frontier where names fall out of the sky and memory does not always agree with itself. This page lists only what is in the current build: one connected map about 768 meters across. More is planned; see the roadmap.

Regions

  • Sable Crossing

    A crossroads village of timber cottages around a flat plaza: a well, market stalls, the longhouse, a notice board and the Wall of Names, where the village writes down who it still remembers.

  • Red Orchard

    Fenced orchard rows on a gentle south-facing slope, a farmhouse and barn, haystacks and a scarecrow that nobody admits to building.

  • Margin Wood

    Dense pine and oak over rolling hills, mossy boulders and a winding trail. The Margin wolves hunt in packs along it.

  • The Misremembered Keep

    A ruined keep on a hill raised sixteen meters above the fields. Three ways in: the gatehouse at the top of the north road ramp, a rubble climb to the collapsed east wall, and the west postern where the Rootcellar tunnel comes up.

  • Rootcellar Nine

    A carved stores tunnel under the west hill, from a cave mouth past Margin Wood up to the keep's west postern. Eight cellars line it. The ninth sits behind a sealed stone door mid-tunnel.

  • Lantern Ford & Memory Forge

    A lantern-lit bridge over the river, a waterwheel, and Orrin Vale's smithy around the Memory Forge, a hearth older than the ford's name.

  • The Namefall Scar

    A crater twelve meters deep, bristling with name-crystals and ringed by name-stones, with a broken throne at its heart. Storms gather here, and so does the Penitent Captain.

Roads: North Road, East Road, West Trail, South Lane and Scar Path. Lantern anchors, where you can bind your respawn point: Crossroads Lantern, Ford Lantern and Keep Gate Lantern.

Weather moves through clear, overcast, rain and the Namefall, and the day turns to night.

People

They have lives of their own, and they remember what you tell them.

  • Mara Venn

    Acting leader of Sable Crossing

    Wants to learn which of her two childhoods really happened. She remembers growing up at the Crossing's well and, just as clearly, on a slow brown river that appears on no map, and she means to find that river once the Crossing can spare her.

  • Orrin Vale

    Blacksmith of the Memory Forge at Lantern Ford

    Wants to hear the truth about his family's death. One of the blades hanging in his forge witnessed it; he can hear the shape of the memory every night but not the words, and he refuses to force it.

Enemies

Every attack is telegraphed before it lands: amber can be guarded, red must be dodged.

  • Brass Penitent: Censer Sweep, Penitent's Lunge and Toll of Judgement.
  • Quillcrown, the Penitent Captain: Censer Sweep, Penitent's Lunge and Toll of Judgement.
  • Margin Wolf: Bite and Pounce.

  • Rarity is not strength. Every bloodline has the same power budget; rarer ones have stranger conditions and steeper costs.
  • Your first draw is free, and you get two free redraws before binding your first character. You may keep any of the three results.
  • Every tenth draw guarantees uncommon or better and every thirtieth guarantees rare or better. Secret bloodlines are never drawn; they are earned through play.

Beyond the draw there are 12 secret bloodlines and 6 convergences. They are never drawn: they are meant to be earned through play, and none can be earned in this build yet. Their names and how to find them stay a secret.

Origins

Your origin gives you a starting advantage and a story pressure. These are playable now:

  • Wayfarer

    Advantage: Better route clues and camp tools. Your compass also shows the bearing of every landmark you have not discovered yet.

    Pressure: No faction initially trusts the player. Mara Venn starts one step of trust behind, and the Crossing waits to see whether you stay.

  • Oathbound

    Advantage: Defensive technique and reputation bonus. Your maximum posture is 10 higher, and you may swear formal oaths in conversation.

    Pressure: Breaking promises creates stronger consequences: a broken promise costs 2x the trust.

  • Seeker

    Advantage: Increased rare-scenario clues. Mara Venn shares what she knows about hidden places at trust 1 instead of 2.

    Pressure: Dangerous curiosities find the player more often. The Penitent Captain at the Namefall Scar notices you from 6 m farther away.

Later: Scavenger, Witness, Outcast, Artisan and Keeper. Character creation shows them, marked as later.
