FIRSTWAKE The Unwritten Frontier

An original open-world action RPG that runs in your browser. Walk out of Sable Crossing, learn to read the Brass Penitent's tells, and explore a connected slice of the Crownless Reach, alone or with one friend.

Play in your browser How co-op works

Free, with no sign-up, no download and no purchases. Best in a desktop browser with WebGL2, using a keyboard and mouse or a gamepad.

In this build

Everything listed here is in the current prototype. The art is temporary: low-poly characters, creatures and buildings generated in code, not finished models. What has been tested so far is listed under Test status.

  • A connected region

    Sable Crossing, the Red Orchard, Margin Wood, the Misremembered Keep with three ways in, the Rootcellar Nine cave, Lantern Ford and its Memory Forge, and the Namefall Scar. They form one streamed, walkable map about 768 meters across, with roads, a river, a day and night cycle, and weather: clear, overcast, rain and the Namefall.

  • Third-person action

    Sprint, jump, dodge through attacks, guard with a perfect-guard window, and lock on. Health, stamina, Focus and posture all matter. Break an enemy's posture and you get exactly one riposte.

  • Two weapons, one technique

    The Crossing Blade has a three-hit light chain and a heavy strike. The Ironweather Longrifle has six rounds, aimed fire and a braced shot. Gravity Chamber makes a space heavy: enemies inside slow down, their wind-ups stretch out, and they lose posture faster.

  • Enemies that warn you

    The Brass Penitent (Censer Sweep, Penitent's Lunge, Toll of Judgement), its elite captain Quillcrown at the Scar, and packs of Margin Wolves that bite, pounce and fall back. Every attack draws a telegraph on the ground first: amber can be guarded, red must be dodged.

  • Bloodlines and origins

    A free Fate Draw with its odds published before you draw. The prototype pool holds the three bloodlines whose gift and drawback are fully playable: Stonewake, Stormbound and Aetherial. Three origins (Wayfarer, Oathbound and Seeker) change how people treat you, what you notice, and what comes after you. Rarity is not power.

  • People who remember

    Mara Venn and Orrin Vale remember what you say. Keep or break a promise to Mara. Earn her tip about the cellar or find the torn map yourself: either way opens the Nine Door. Your personal Chronicle records what happened to you, and the realm's Chronicle records what happened to everyone.

  • Co-op for two

    Host a realm and share a code with one friend. The host's browser runs the world; each of you keeps your own character, choices and loot.

  • Local saves

    Progress is saved in this browser, with rolling backups and recovery from damaged saves. Export a save file to carry it to another device.

  • Readable and adjustable

    Remappable keys, reduced motion and flashing, a camera-shake slider, high-contrast and deuteranopia telegraph colors, text and subtitle scaling, speaker labels, and on-screen cues for sounds.

Not in this build

The other eighteen Fate Draw bloodlines, the other five origins, Wakesmithing at the Memory Forge, signature techniques and synthesis, branching story chains, a death penalty, finished character models, and tested phone and tablet support. See the roadmap.

How co-op works

  1. Host. One player picks Host on the title screen. The game opens their realm and shows an invite code like FW-7KQ2-M9XD-R4TB-WN3C.
  2. Share the code with one friend, any way you like.
  3. Join. The friend picks Join, chooses their own character and enters the code.
  4. Play. You share the same world, enemies, weather and time of day. Each of you keeps your own character, choices, loot and Chronicle.

Good to know

  • Two players: the host and one friend.
  • The host's tab must stay open. The host's browser runs the world. If the host switches away from the tab, the game pauses for both of you; if the host closes it, the session ends. There is no host migration.
  • Dropped connections: if the friend's connection drops, the game keeps trying to reconnect them to the same character for 60 seconds.
  • How the connection is made: the free PeerJS signaling service introduces the two browsers, then game traffic flows directly between them over WebRTC. When a direct link is impossible, PeerJS's free public relay is tried. It is best-effort and not guaranteed.
  • Which networks work: testing starts with two windows on one PC, then two devices on one home network. Across the internet it depends on both networks: strict firewalls at workplaces and schools, and some mobile carriers, can block it. Results are listed under Test status.
  • Trust: the host's browser is the referee and saves can be edited, so co-op is for friends. It is not built for rankings, trading or anti-cheat.

Saves

  • They stay in this browser

    Saves live in this browser on this device. Nothing is uploaded, and there are no accounts or cloud sync.

  • Moving to another device

    Open Import / Export from the menu to download a save file, then import it on the other device or browser. Import shows what it will change and asks before overwriting anything.

  • Backups and recovery

    Each save keeps five rolling backups. If a save is damaged, the game sets the damaged copy aside and restores the newest good backup, and it tells you when it does.

  • What can erase them

    Clearing this site's data, some browser clean-up tools, private or incognito windows, and browsers that remove data from sites you have not visited for a while (Safari can do this after 7 days). The game asks the browser to keep its storage when it can, but an exported save file is the only copy you fully control.

In co-op the host's browser keeps the realm (the shared world and its history), and each player's browser keeps their own character.

    Roadmap Future: not in this build

    This is where FIRSTWAKE is headed. None of it is playable yet, there are no dates, and the order may change. The Crownless Reach gets deeper before any new region is added.

    1. Identity

      Signature techniques that grow from how you actually fight, chosen evolutions, deeper NPC memory, Fate Marks and technique synthesis.

    2. Branching stories

      Story chains with shared and personal consequences, including a rare scenario with several ways to find it and resolve it.

    3. Wakesmithing

      Forging at the Memory Forge: material choices, provenance, burdens, repairs and relics that evolve.

    4. A wider Fate Draw

      More of the 21 drawable bloodlines, more origins, more weapon families and techniques, and secret bloodlines earned through play.

    5. Presentation

      Finished rigged characters and animation, richer scenery and music, performance measured on more hardware, and tested gamepad and mobile support.

    Public servers, rankings, trading and global first-discovery records would need trusted online services. They are not part of this private co-op version.

    Credits and licenses

    FIRSTWAKE's world, characters, story and game design are original. All art is generated in code and all sound is synthesized live in the browser: the game ships no third-party art, models, textures, fonts, music or sound files.

    No AI service runs while you play. Every rule and every piece of content is local to the game.

    Network services are used only when you host or join co-op: the PeerJS cloud signaling server, Google's public STUN server, and PeerJS's free TURN relay.

    Privacy: this page sets no cookies and has no analytics or trackers. The game stores its saves on your device.
